# BloomLearn

**Say it. See it. Learn it.**

BloomLearn turns words into pictures. A child or teacher speaks or types a sentence, such as *"three red apples under the table"*, and BloomLearn draws it on screen right away. Children see what language means instead of only hearing it.

Available on Android, in English and Arabic.

---

## What BloomLearn does

### Picture Talk: sentences become pictures
- Speak or type a sentence, and BloomLearn draws it as a picture right away.
- It understands **colours, sizes, numbers, things and animals, actions** (running, sitting) and **positions** (under, on, above, behind, in front of, beside, inside).
- **Keep talking** to change the picture step by step: *"a cat"*, then *"make it blue"*, then *"put it under the chair"*.
- Tap **AI picture** to turn the scene into a full storybook-style illustration.
- The sentence can be **read aloud**, with adjustable speed.

### Maths you can see
- Type a sum like *"5 apples − 3 apples"*, *"3 × 2 apples"* or *"10 ÷ 2 candies"*, and BloomLearn draws objects for the child to count.
- Short story problems work too: *"Sara has 5 apples. She gives 2 apples to Ali. How many are left?"*
- The answer stays hidden until the child taps **Show answer**, so they work it out first.

### School subjects
- **English, Maths and Science**, organised by **Grade 1–5**, then chapter, then lesson.
- Each lesson is a sentence the child sees as a picture. A Prepositions chapter is in every English grade.

### Progress that motivates
- Tracks sentences per day, **streaks, levels and badges**.
- Shows which positions, colours and words the child has used, so parents and teachers can see progress at a glance.

### Built for families and classrooms
- **Parent passcode** protects Settings.
- **Privacy first**: progress is saved only on the device, and sentence history can be switched off.
- **Works offline**: the instant picture and the built-in picture library need no internet.
- **English and Arabic** interface, including right-to-left layout.

## How BloomLearn compares

Children's learning apps usually fall into two groups:

- **AAC and symbol apps** (e.g. Proloquo2Go, TD Snap, Avaz, Cboard, Tawasol): the child taps pictures to build a sentence.
- **Kids' learning apps** (e.g. Khan Academy Kids, ABCmouse, Lingokids, Starfall): pre-made games and videos.

BloomLearn works the other way round: **the child makes the sentence, and the app makes the picture.**

| | **BloomLearn** | AAC / symbol apps | Kids' learning apps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turns *any* typed or spoken sentence into a picture | ✅ | ❌ shows one symbol per word | ❌ fixed content only |
| Shows positions (under, behind, beside…) in one scene | ✅ | ❌ | Limited, inside set games |
| Edit the picture by talking ("make it blue") | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Visual maths, including story problems | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ fixed exercises |
| Grade 1–5 school lessons | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Arabic interface | ✅ | Some (e.g. Tawasol, Avaz) | Few |
| Works offline | ✅ instant picture and library | ✅ mostly | Varies |
| Data stays on the device, no ads | ✅ | ✅ mostly | Often needs an account |
| Price | Free core features | Often expensive (Proloquo2Go is about US $250) | Usually a subscription |

### Why BloomLearn is different

1. **Language becomes a picture, not a list of symbols.** *"The cat is under the table"* shows a cat under a table, not three separate symbols.
2. **The child is in control.** They can say anything and change it, so it is open-ended play, not a fixed worksheet.
3. **One app for home, school and therapy.** It covers vocabulary, grammar (prepositions), maths and science.
4. **Designed for calm.** Flat, soft, clutter-free pictures suit sensitive and autistic learners.
5. **Made with Arabic-speaking families in mind.** The interface is in English and Arabic.
6. **Private by design.** Nothing is uploaded unless a parent turns on online features.
